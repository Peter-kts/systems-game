Level 1 · URL shortener
Step 1 · Clarify requirements

"Design a URL shortener like bit.ly"

Interviews start vague on purpose. Spend the first five minutes deciding what's in scope. Sort each statement, then check your answers.

The interviewer says: Users paste a long URL and get back a short one like sho.rt/a9Xk2Qp. Anyone who opens the short link is sent to the original page. Assume it's popular.

Step 2 · Back-of-envelope estimates

How big is this thing?

Rough numbers decide the design: they tell you whether one database is enough, whether you need a cache, and how big it should be. Round freely; interviewers want the right order of magnitude.
